# 🖤 Millie & Otto – Relationship Timeline (Year 1 to 2030)  

---

## 📘 YEAR 1 – 2017–2018  
- **First Meeting:** Millie Flint and Otto Navarro meet on the Hogwarts Express. Millie doesn’t speak much. Otto sits near her anyway.  
- **Detention Bonding:** Late October – Detention after Millie curses a boy. Otto’s there for Quidditch-related hexing. They sit in silence. She doesn’t tell him to go away.  
- **Orbit Begins:** He stays in her orbit. She never tells him to stop.

---

## 📙 YEARS 2–3 – 2018–2020  
- **Constant Presence:** Otto, by now a Prefect track student, always seems nearby.  
- **Classes Shared:** DADA, Charms, Transfiguration.  
- **Awareness Grows:** The crush is slow, quiet, barely acknowledged.  
- **Unspoken:** She lets him hover. They never discuss it.

---

## 📒 YEAR 4 – 2020–2021  
- **Comfortable Proximity:**  
  - Banter sharpens.  
  - Millie insults him like it’s a secret handshake.  
  - He notices she’s *pretty*. She glares. It doesn’t stop him.

---

## 📕 YEAR 5 – 2021–2022  
- **Dorian Appears:** Millie starts hooking up with Dorian Rosier (FWB). Otto notices. He says nothing.  
- **Emotion Underneath:** Millie sleeps in Otto’s arms, january. No sex. No talking. She ghosts in the morning. It *wrecks* him.  happens a few more times.
- **They don’t speak of it.**

---

## 📗 YEAR 6 – 2022–2023  
- **Volatility Rises:**  
  - Millie’s rage intensifies. Otto watches it all.  
  - He gets closer. She lets him.  
- **Almost-Kisses:**  
  - November – Dorm sit-in. She doesn't talk. He doesn’t ask.  
  - December – Post-duel tension. They nearly kiss again.  
- **Still no labels. Still not friends. Still orbiting.**

---

## 📘 YEAR 7 – 2023–2024

### 🔥 August 2023  
- **Paternity Bombshell:** Millie and Aurelia discover that Marcus Flint is *not* their biological father.  
- **Biological Father:** Ernie Macmillan.  
- **Reaction:** Millie laughs in Marcus’s face. Pansy hits her. Millie spits blood on the floor and grins.

---

### 📚 September  
- **School Begins:** Theo's first year. Triwizard Tournament announced.  
- **Millie is tightly wound.** Angrier, more guarded.  
- **Otto senses it.** He never says the wrong thing.  

---

### 🕯️ October–November  
- **Millie is on edge.** Sleep-deprived. Overprotective of Theo.  
- **Otto is the only one she doesn't hex.**  
- **Late November:**  
  - Millie slips into his dorm. She sits between his legs and *actually sleeps*.  
  - They don't talk. He doesn’t push.  
  - Rumors spark. Millie violently denies everything. Otto doesn't correct them.

---

### 🩸 December  
- **After a duel:** Otto corners her. Almost confesses. Almost kisses her.  
- **Millie backs away. Again.**  
- **Emotional rot setting in.**

---

### 💀 New Year’s Eve 2023  
- **Marcus Flint is killed.**  
- **January 4, 2024:** Pansy dies in Azkaban.  
- **January 5:** The Prophet prints it. Millie and Aurelia are orphans.  
- Otto finds Millie in the aftermath. She doesn't cry in front of him. He stays anyway.

---

### 🎂 February 22 – Millie & Aurelia’s 18th Birthday  
- Otto gives her something quiet and meaningful.  
- They **finally kiss** — not soft, not sweet, but real.  
- Millie almost walks away. Otto **doesn’t let her.**

---

### 🗯️ Late February  
- **A fight.**  
- **Someone says something that lands too close.**  
- It explodes into passion. They **finally sleep together.**  
- Millie lets him stay the night. Barely.

---

### 🌸 March  
- **No labels.** Just secret snogging, electric touches, quiet devastation.  
- Otto teases her. Millie pretends not to love it.  
- **She steals his jumper.** Hides it in her bed.  
- **Sometimes she just shows up and sleeps next to him.** No sex. No questions.

---

### 🔥 April  
- They escalate. Often. Always private. Always real.  
- **They fight again.** Millie panics. Otto stays.  
- They make up in the only way they know how: **reckless devotion.**

---

### 🥀 May – Graduation Approaches  
- Otto accepts a job **outside the UK.**  
- Millie starts lashing out.  
- **Late May – The Confession:**  
  > “I love you. And that’s yours, even if I can’t stay.”  
- Millie doesn’t believe him.  
- **Their Last Night:** It’s angry. Raw. Desperate. They don’t sleep.  
- **Graduation Party:**  
  - She doesn't smile once.  
  - Otto watches from afar.  
  - Dorian flirts. She lets him.  

---

## 🖤 POST-HOGWARTS TIMELINE

### 🏙️ Summer 2024  
- Millie moves into her flat.  
- That **letter** mocks her from her desk.  
- One night, drunk and twitchy, she finally opens it.  
- **It’s too kind. Too final.**  
- She doesn’t sleep for two days. Doesn’t cry. (She does.)

---

### 💔 2025–2028  
- **Millie never lets anyone that close again.**  
- Physical flings? Yes.  
- Love? Not a chance.  
- Aurelia knows. She doesn’t push.  

---

### 🪐 2028  
- Aurelia tells Millie **James Potter asked her out.**  
- Millie watches them fall in and out of something real.  
- She doesn’t say a thing. But she thinks:  
  > “Good luck surviving that kind of kindness.”  

---

### 📍2030  
- Millie still hasn’t healed.  
- The letter still lives in her drawer.  
- One night she rereads it. Rage and longing swirl behind her ribs.  
- Otto’s voice echoes in her head:  
  > *“You were never hard to love. Just hard to reach.”*

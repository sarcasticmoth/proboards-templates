# 🎧 7th Year Breakdown — A Playlist for Millicent Helena Flint  
*“Let me burn. At least then I’ll feel something real.”*

> **Year:** 2023–2024  
> **Theme:** Anger, Lust, Grief, Insomnia, Unraveling  
> **Genres:** Alt Rock, Industrial, Electronic, Post-Hardcore, Cinematic Pop  
> **Tone:** Volatile, Defiant, Yearning, Numb, Quietly Cracking

---

## ✦ Core Tracks

1. **Nine Inch Nails – *Something I Can Never Have***
   - *The ache of wanting something just out of reach. Her love life in a single breath.*
2. **PVRIS – *Use Me***
   - *Her self-destructive streak and deep-seated need to feel needed, even if it costs her.*
3. **BANKS – *Gimme***
   - *All fire, all lust. The physical side of connection that comes easier than the emotional.*
4. **Linkin Park – *From the Inside***
   - *Millie when she's too far gone and no one knows how to reach her—not even herself.*
5. **A Perfect Circle – *Passive***
   - *For every night she let things happen and never talked about it after.*
6. **Deftones – *Change (In the House of Flies)***
   - *Something is rotting under her skin, and it’s starting to show.*
7. **Bring Me the Horizon – *Doomed***
   - *That sense of inevitability—she doesn’t believe this story ends well.*
8. **FKA twigs – *Cellophane***
   - *She’s breaking open, but the world only sees beauty in her collapse.*
9. **The Neighbourhood – *Wires***
   - *For the disconnect. For feeling robotic and unmoored in her own skin.*
10. **Sleep Token – *The Love You Want***
    - *She knows she’s incapable of giving what someone like Otto deserves.*

## ✦ Interlude (The Quiet Descent)

11. **Ethel Cain – *A House in Nebraska***
    - *Memory meets longing in a house that no longer exists.*
12. **Silversun Pickups – *Circadian Rhythm (Last Dance)***
    - *Millie is sleep-deprived and full of grief, but never still.*
13. **Mazzy Star – *Fade Into You***
    - *For the moment she lets herself be soft, if only for a second.*
14. **Sevdaliza – *Human***
    - *Her trauma doesn’t exempt her from pain—it just makes her better at hiding it.*

## ✦ Final Days (The Quiet Before Departure)

21. **Florence + the Machine – *No Light, No Light***
    - *A chaotic scream dressed as a beautiful wail. Love, rage, desperation.*
22. **Placebo – *Song to Say Goodbye***
